# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee data to uncover key insights related to workforce trends, employee attrition, and organizational performance. The dashboard is designed to help HR teams make data-driven decisions by visualizing critical HR metrics in an interactive and user-friendly format.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze employee attrition and identify key factors influencing it
* Monitor workforce demographics (age, gender, department, etc.)
* Track employee performance and job satisfaction
* Provide actionable insights to improve employee retention

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset includes employee-related information such as:

* Employee ID, Age, Gender
* Department, Job Role, Salary
* Years at Company, Experience
* Job Satisfaction, Performance Rating
* Attrition Status

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and visualization
* **Excel / CSV** – Data source
* **SQL (optional)** – Data extraction and transformation

---

## 📊 Key Dashboard Features

* **Attrition Analysis**:

  * Attrition rate by department, age group, and gender
* **Employee Demographics**:

  * Distribution based on age, gender, and education
* **Salary Insights**:

  * Salary trends across job roles and departments
* **Performance Metrics**:

  * Employee performance ratings and satisfaction levels
* **Interactive Filters**:

  * Department, gender, and experience-based filtering

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Higher attrition observed in specific departments and lower salary bands
* Employees with lower job satisfaction are more likely to leave
* Mid-level experience employees show higher turnover rates
* Salary and work-life balance strongly impact retention

---

## 🚀 Outcome

The dashboard provides HR teams with a clear understanding of workforce dynamics and helps in:

* Reducing employee turnover
* Improving employee satisfaction
* Making strategic HR decisions

---

## 📌 How to Use

1. Open the Power BI file (.pbix)
2. Interact with filters and visuals
3. Explore insights across different employee segments
